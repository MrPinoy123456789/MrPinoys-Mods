# Pocket Dungeons: the next roadmap

## 1. Departures from existing conventions

Proposed for owner review, not permission to implement. These departures are explicit; several reconcile changes already present in the working tree rather than introduce new ones.

- **Retain the shipped 1 to 100 ladder, not restore the brief's 1 to 25.** M10 and `PocketDungeonsConfig.keystoneMaxLevel` already use 100. Preserve existing saves and access gates; judge the climb by new decisions and museum materials, not more numerical tiers. Do not add another cap increase.
- **Retain the situations floor loop instead of restoring five to eight strictly forward rooms.** Keep the safe/staging split, backtracking within a floor, and three floors per safe visit initially. This preserves scarce-tool decisions from `SITUATIONS_SPEC` sections 6 and 12. Restore the silent, physical arrival home at the final safe door, not the old room's mid-run accessibility. The three run doors remain in staging, adjacent to home, not inside the decorated room.
- **Finish replacing timed failure with omen.** Contrary to the brief, `RunTimer.tick` still sets overtime and `Instances.onTick` still calls `RunLifecycle.expireTimedOut`. Remove this hidden second punishment from ordinary floors. Keep Pocket2's explicitly time-limited identity separate. Bank one keystone level per completed safe-visit interval in the low/middle omen bands, zero in the high band, as situations sections 5.4 and 12.4 intend, rather than the current per-floor door-step awards. Existing earned levels are never revoked. Voluntary abandonment and restart penalties require the owner decisions in section 7.
- **Retain optional public discovery and restore private calling cards alongside it.** M20 deleted cards and shipped a directory. Public listings remain explicit opt-in with existing flags preserved; private rooms cannot be searched or enumerated. New, revocable cards spread by hand. This deliberately narrows `VISION` 3.1.1's universal no-browse rule to the private channel: public rooms seed encounters on small servers, cards carry relationships beyond them. Never silently reactivate pre-M20 cards.
- **Replace two Cube recipes, not the bag-as-class rule.** Retire the class-changing `BAG_OVERRIDE` in favour of a bounded tool-supply recipe; replace `DOUBLE_KEY` with an ordinary dungeon-supplied catalyst for a longer floor. Authoritative keystones are reconciled state, not sacrificial currency. The eight chosen classes stay locked until full reset, and recipes never reroll a class or refill its whole kit. M66 specifies pending-tag migration.
- **Retire Molten's exclusive-lava claim.** Plumber already carries lava and situations already contain it. Recognise those faucets, preserve Molten through its hazard/resource arrangement rather than exclusivity, and validate the supply chain. No new always-on home lava dispenser is proposed.
- **Change the discovery floor, not the secrecy of undiscovered combinations.** Add a guaranteed early opportunity to try the Cube and a personal record of successful discoveries. This supersedes `DIALOGS_SPEC`'s no-hint stance, not `VISION` 5.4: ingredients and a first experiment are legible; the recipe catalogue and adventure graph remain undisclosed.
- **Replace enum-bound identities with namespaced data definitions.** Affixes, author-facing roles, bags and Cube recipes become extensible ordinary Java maps over bounded engine operations. This is not a custom Minecraft registry and does not require a client. Existing codec fields remain readable and superseded fields remain retained on first migration.
- **Mixin policy follows this planning mandate, not the obsolete one-class cap in `CONVENTIONS.md` and discovery trap 9.** No new mixin is scheduled. Retain only `CustomClickMixin`, targeting `ServerCommonPacketListenerImpl.handleCustomClickAction` at cancellable `HEAD`, because the verified dialog event gap still exists. Reconfirm target and namespace/thread guards on version bumps. Any later proposal must name its class, method, injection point, exhausted alternatives and update cost before approval.

**Unchanged:** server-only release, vanilla assets/items/blocks/sounds, no new custom registry entries, self-sufficiency, silence about room movement, 26.2 jar verification, slang mechanical voice, codec migration discipline and house punctuation. No new in-run buildcraft system, drafted abilities, synergy engine, PvP, monetisation or mandatory lore. Existing trims and extract/imbue are retained for save compatibility and audited for scarcity leakage, not expanded into a power-progression tree. Ordinary Minecraft tool placement is a situation solution, not drafted buildcraft.

## 2. What "best" means for Pocket Dungeons

The bar is the loop in `VISION` 3.1 working socially: a player recognises another player's room, asks about something in it, learns a route or experiment, and takes a door to obtain their own story. The generator earns that moment by producing problems with several useful answers, not merely more coordinates. `VISION` 6 succeeds when another server can publish a recognisably different dungeon experience without a Java fork.

Release evidence, not promises of popularity:

- A new vanilla-client player can choose a bag, preview and finish a floor, bank a safe visit, place a run-earned object at home and invite someone to see it without an operator explaining the loop. The final homecoming is walked and unexplained.
- Repeated play produces different resource decisions at the same key level. Across a fixed twelve-floor review set per theme, record situations engaged/skipped, repeated ordered room pairs and tools actually spent; a recolour with the same decisions does not count as new content depth.
- A person outside this implementation team adds a namespaced affix, theme, role and Cube recipe, reloads them, diagnoses one deliberate error and upgrades their pack using only published tooling. No source checkout or compilation.
- Inventory custody, room preservation and rewards have failure-injection evidence. Operators have a measured concurrency envelope, not an unqualified player-count claim.
- Pilot hosts observe repeat visits, hand-traded cards and player-taught discoveries. Report the denominator and population size; no retention claim can be inferred from code or an arranged demonstration. No global rankings or automated public activity feed.

## 7. Open questions for the mod owner

1. **Will you ratify the retained 100-level, bag-class, three-floor loop and the restored physical final homecoming?** This preserves shipped saves and situations while recovering `VISION` 4. Returning to 25 or the original short corridor instead needs an explicit level/access migration and would replace, not polish, M55 to M61.
2. **What are voluntary abandonment and restart meant to cost?** Recommendation: process restart safely ends the interval, returns custody and charges no penalty; voluntary quit may deplete only under an explicitly disclosed rule. Resuming the exact altered floor is nicer but requires persistent world/state checkpoints, not regeneration from a seed. Resolve before M65; no implementation default is assumed.
3. **Is +1 per successful safe visit the intended climb, or should Greater doors bank additional levels?** The spec says interval settlement; current code banks door steps per floor. +1 preserves a meaningful interval but lengthens a 100-level climb. Greater doors would then need sufficient access/treasure value for fuel, not an unexplained lost advantage. Choose from measured M67 session lengths, with provisional policy fixed before M65.
4. **Should public listings coexist with revocable hand-traded private cards?** Recommended for density plus folklore. Pure cards maximise intentional audiences but make a small server harder to discover; directory-only is simpler but loses the private social object. Confirm card forwarding, revocation and offline visitation policy before M75.
5. **How much session time can your actual test group commit between safe visits?** Keep three floors initially, then measure. Shorter intervals make banked progress accessible; longer intervals strengthen resource decisions but punish interrupted evenings. Name who can perform the M67 uncoached and six-player checks, and who owns sign-off.
6. **Will you approve replacing BAG_OVERRIDE and DOUBLE_KEY, including an explicit legacy refund policy?** The replacements preserve class identity and avoid spending reconciled keystones. Keeping the original designs needs an argued class-switch exception and a real secondary-keystone ownership model, not only fixing tag order.
7. **What deployment is the supported first release, and who are its independent hosts/authors?** Vanilla clients plus server-only content is the strongest initial promise. Existing client-required content packs and inventory managers widen the audience but add real compatibility obligations. Agree the tested set, target hardware and pilot contacts before M72/M77; the mod cannot create their population.
8. **What is the intended place of existing gear, trims and extracted powers after scarcity is enforced?** Recommend preserving owned items and station functionality, but not allowing persistent kits to trivialise new runs or adding a power tree. If safe-room gear may enter every interval, situations must be balanced for it and the no-buildcraft identity loses force. Resolve the admission rule before M65; never silently delete or invalidate players' earned gear.

The first release should be held to M77's evidence, not M78's feature count. A reliable room worth showing, a dungeon worth discussing and a format another author can use are the product; everything after them has to earn its place.
