Pocket Dungeons terminal: to-scale mockup

The 12 by 5 terminal wall as a player sees it from the room, at 72 px per block. The tabs, arrows and reward icons can be clicked, just like in game. Item icons are drawn as placeholders. Where this picture and 01-SPEC.md differ, the spec's numbers win.

Wall blocks: black = black concrete, pale = sea lantern backlight (lit only on an open act's Objectives page), bottom row = polished blackstone tab bar. Text sizes follow 0.025 blocks per text pixel per unit of scale.
