## Bugs

- **PD-161 (Medium): the door board advertises ore that is not there.**
  Copper Works declares `nodePalette` [iron_ore, coal_ore], so the sheets
  promise "Mine the walls for iron and coal" and the pay line lists ore,
  but the dungeon has no `hiddenOre` section and its rooms expose no
  palette blocks: all four floors journaled `nodes_total` 0. The player
  predicted it from the fiction before the data confirmed it. Fix
  direction: give ore dungeons a hiddenOre section or an
  ensureResourceRooms-style guarantee, or suppress the ore words when the
  plan holds none.
- **PD-162 (Medium): the scrap model reads as two competing numbers.**
  Compass lore "Scrap 2/5 to chart 1 (2 held)" computes the next chart off
  the spendable pool (`chartLevel(scrap)+1`) while the compass level shows
  `highestCharts` (12, migrated). A level-12 player is told he is working
  "to chart 1". Side-branch refusals ("2 scrap short") name the gap, never
  the holding. Six player questions burned on this seam in ten minutes.
- **PD-163 (Medium, harness): intermittent "No player was found" on
  lemon_reply/lemon_say while the player is online.** ~00:36:40 a reply
  failed and the ask hit `lemon unanswered` at 00:37:23 (45 s fallback);
  ~00:50:5x reply and say both failed, a retry about a minute later worked.
  `status` showed him online throughout. A player-lookup race on the
  remote path is the likely shape.
- **PD-164 (High): barred_vault is broken and exploitable.** Player
  report: "totally wrong, I can pull the stuff out of the hopper, there's
  a double door in the middle of the room with some redstone and none of
  it does anything and looks like it's missing inner walls." The SpurSpecs
  filter hopper holds 4x64 trial keys as a vanilla item filter; if they
  leave with the player they redeem as emeralds at the clear line under
  J7. The lock mechanism itself is dead.
- **PD-165 (Medium): first-clear milestone title not seen.** Asked right
  after two first clears: "I don't think I see the dungeon completion
  message, maybe I just didn't notice it." Either the "<Dungeon> cleared"
  title plus act-progress subtitle does not fire live, or it is too quiet
  to notice. `dungeon_finished` journaled both times.
- **PD-166 (Low, admin tooling): no way to grant scrap.** `addScrap` is
  only reachable from floor pay, `spendScrap` only from door commits.
  The owner asked in-session for 60 scrap and there is no command; side
  branches are also untestable for migrated players whose pools start
  empty. A `dungeon admin scrap` would unblock both.
