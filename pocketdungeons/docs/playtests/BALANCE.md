## Changes 2026-10-02 (playtest 2026-10-02-1 fix batch)

- **Stack caps removed.** Loot tables used to set `minecraft:max_stack_size` to 8 on blocks, torches and arrows. Added with no evidence they were needed; they made tagged and untagged stacks fail to merge. All 237 sets are gone and a stack the pack still carries with the old component is put back to its vanilla size on entry (`InventorySwap.withoutLegacyStackCap`). If hoarding blocks, torches or arrows ever becomes a problem, the old cap was 8 via a `minecraft:set_components` loot function; that is the lever to restore.
- **Armour drops.** Tier 3 spawners equipped mobs from vanilla trial chamber tables (full trimmed armour). They now use `equipment/tier_3_*` (untrimmed iron and diamond, 45 to 55 percent empty per slot). Worn armour drops at least `DungeonDrops.ARMOUR_DROP_CHANCE` 0.08 per slot (was 0.2). Weapons keep 0.2, bows 0.05.
- **Mob variety.** Themeless rooms draw one of three narrow families (undead, bones, spiders: two related mobs each) chosen per cell; `chaoticSpawnerChance` 0.05 keeps the broad mixed pool as a rare explicit chaotic spawner.
- **Echo shards.** One guaranteed per full 3-floor interval (`echoShardsPerInterval` 1, whichever door the last floor used; a partial interval keeps the old free door payout), `echoShardFloorChance` 0.5 per member per cleared floor, `echoShardOrdealChance` 0.25 per player in the room when an Ordeal resolves. Expected: about 1 + 1.5 + a fraction of Ordeals per interval. Tune after the next session.
- **Silence.** No longer blocks consumables. Every `silencedConsumablesPerOmen` (3) consumables used raises the omen by 1 (source SILENCE, with a warden sound and a line).
- **Dead-end fountain.** `fountainChance` 0.15 per dead-end cell with no spawner; boon is a full heal, full food or `fountainOmenRelief` (2) omen off the floor.
- **Loot.** Ender chests in tier 3 and 4 chests and vaults became 8 obsidian (same weight).
- **New kit.** Guard's Bag: stone sword, shield, 4 bread.
- **Mending.** Removed from random loot (enchant tag `pocketdungeons:random_loot`, non-treasure plus a few treasure picks) and from rerolls. The librarian adds it for `lockInEmeralds` (32, unlock level 5); locked gear is exempt from salvage and reroll. Emerald sink; tune the price after the next session.
- **Gamble block gone.** The copper chest that opened the gamble duplicated the blacksmith. Run Storage (27 slots per player, returned on close) is the ender chest placed in a run; the bag chest became the waxed oxidized copper chest.
