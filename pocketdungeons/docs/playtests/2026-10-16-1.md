## Design feedback (not bugs, owner-originated)

One continuous thread about the economy past mid-game:

- "merchants seem to be less relevant, their stock is useless in later acts
  (act 3+)". When asked what a deep merchant should sell: "I don't know."
- "mending is too powerful and should be removed, currently it's sold by the
  home merchant. that way tools armour weapons become material sinks again".
  Evidence from his own pack: "all my gear has mending on it now so I don't
  need materials anymore". Inventory snapshot confirms: diamond/netherite with
  enchants across the board, repair materials unspent.
- "need more well designed sinks, maybe later acts introduce new obstacles
  that require some kind of sink". His candidate: "water and sponges could be
  something". When asked for more ideas back: brainstorm gave hot floors that
  boil buckets dry, ender rooms that open for pearls, a shrine that eats a
  spare tool for a shortcut, food spoiling deep.
- "make enchantments and trim very rare to spawn on items (only as floor
  rewards) so players need to customize their gear themselves"; "enchantment
  books are still okay though" (the carve-out); "should be able to spend
  emeralds for xp potions in home merchant" (closes the loop: plain drops,
  buy XP, enchant yourself).
- Mechanism rooms do not read: "many of the rooms that use hoppers or chest
  contents are not intuitive and look weird ... the one that requires food to
  be thrown in a hopper makes no sense from a ui perspective" (the_altar).
  And: "some rooms have chains of hoppers when the player can just place the
  item directly in the final chest" (sorting_floor's channel is dressing, not
  a puzzle).
- Mode idea: "a gamemode where the player is given a big 4x4 chunk room and
  have to survive waves". A wave arena; the mine guardians are the nearest
  bones for it.
