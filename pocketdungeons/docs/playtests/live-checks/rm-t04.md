## Phased finales, Act 2 (Cow Pits, Deepslate, Frostworks) (2026-10-19)
- **Status:** owed (built, never played; listed here so the knowledge pack carries it)
- **Changed:** The Act 2 finales run in three phases (backlog F2). Cow Pits (feed Specimen 0): it fights alone, at 2/3 health the herd charges, at 1/3 the herd grows. Deepslate (Deep Sentry): the Heard meter is deaf at first, fills on noise from 2/3, and a full meter sends the whelp only from 1/3. Frostworks (Jack Frost): no cold at first, freeze ticks from 2/3, warm spots shrink to half from 1/3.
- **Do:** One account per dungeon, compass 2 or higher. Play each Act 2 final floor and deal damage steadily. Cow Pits: feed the pedestal cow, then fight. Deepslate: make noise (bow, sprint) in phase two and watch the meter fill. Frostworks: stand away from the braziers in phase two, then at the edge of the old warm radius in phase three.
- **Pass:** At each bound the boss freezes for 3 seconds with a gold title, a stunned sound and no damage taken. The named mechanic arms only after the stagger. Cow Pits: cows stay placid until the second title. Deepslate: a meter full in phase two holds until the third title, then the heartbeat tell, 3 seconds later darkness and a whelp. Frostworks: wind and an action bar line before the first freeze tick; in phase three the edge of the old warm spot no longer thaws. The pad opens when the boss and the rest are dead. An unfed Cow Pits finishes with no fight.
- **Fail signs:** A phase skipped, a mechanic live before its title, a whelp or freeze with no warning, herd cows that never attack after Specimen 0 dies, the pad staying shut, or Jack Frost's name differing from the plan's Frost Warden (known: the code name is kept).
