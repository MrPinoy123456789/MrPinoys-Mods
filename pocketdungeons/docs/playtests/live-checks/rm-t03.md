## Act 1 phased finales (2026-10-19)
- **Status:** owed (built, never played; listed here so the knowledge pack carries it)
- **Changed:** The final floor of Mineshaft, Lush Caves, Infestation, The Kennels and Ossuary is a three phase boss fight (roadmap T03, DECISIONS 2026-10-15). Each boss staggers at two thirds and one third of its health (title, crit particles, a ravager stun sound, about 3 seconds invulnerable), and a killing blow can no longer skip a phase. Mineshaft: The Pit Boss (a zombie), lights fail after phase two, the rest of the shaft arrives as glowing cave spiders in phase three. Lush Caves: The Great Drip (giant slime), the potion drip quickens in phase two, its first split is doubled in phase three. Infestation: The Queen stays on the surface in phase one, burrows and plants ripening bricks from phase two, bricks ripen twice as fast in phase three. The Kennels: The Alpha, the handler raises his arm in phase two (kill him to cancel), the pack grows and glows red in phase three if he lived. Ossuary: The Sexton fights in the open with every coffin sealed, coffins crack as a member walks past in phase two, a skipped coffin opens by itself in phase three.
- **Do:** One account per dungeon, on the final floor of each (any compass). Clear the floor's spawners and wait for the countdown. Hit the boss hard (a strong weapon, or several players) so a single blow carries it under a bound. Watch each bound, then finish the boss. Repeat in a party of two to check scaling. Leave the Kennels handler alive in one run and kill him during phase two in another.
- **Pass:** Phase one is a plain fight with no mechanic. Crossing each bound shows a gold title with a gray sub line, a particle burst and a stun sound, and the boss cannot be hurt for about 3 seconds. A hard hit never jumps two phases at once: the bar stops at two thirds, then one third. Mineshaft: a creak, then the lights go out at least 2 seconds later; torches by the pad stay lit; the cave spiders glow. Lush: potions fall faster after phase two; the boss splits into six where it used to give about three. Infestation: no burrow before phase two; bricks go mossy, stone, infested, then burst; phase three bricks burst in roughly half the time; the Queen is never hittable while burrowed and is never frozen mid burrow by a stagger. Kennels: the horn and raised arm at phase two; killing the handler before the pack grows keeps the wolves small; if he lives, the wolves grow after at least 5 seconds and glow red; killing him afterwards shrinks them. Ossuary: no coffin opens in phase one even beside one; phase two coffins crack with a sound and dust for about 2.5 seconds before the lid breaks, one at a time; phase three opens a far coffin by itself after about 15 seconds. The pad opens when everything is dead; torches and lids come back.
- **Fail signs:** A boss that dies without all its phases showing; a phase title with no stagger or a stagger that never ends; lights that stay out after the fight; a coffin that never opens after the Sexton dies (soft lock); a Queen stuck invulnerable or visible under the floor; the red wolf team left after leaving the floor; the pad stuck shut with no mob alive; a disconnect.
