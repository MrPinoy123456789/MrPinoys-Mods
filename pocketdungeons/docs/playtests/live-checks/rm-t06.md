## One Mineshaft trip that goes down a story (2026-10-19)
- **Status:** owed (built, never played; listed here so the knowledge pack carries it)
- **Changed:** Vertical floors phase 2 (roadmap task T06). The Main Drift, Side Gallery and Deep Face floors of the Mineshaft now put one stair room on the way through and the rooms after it sit one story lower. Nothing else in any dungeon changed.
- **Do:** One account. Enter a Mineshaft and reach the Main Drift (or Side Gallery, or Deep Face). Walk the main path until a room with a staircase going down into the floor. Take it. Fight a few mobs on the stairs. Carry on to the exit pad and finish the floor. Do it once more on a Deep Face floor to see the finale a story down. Leave Timber Adit and Mine Mouth alone; they stay flat.
- **Pass:** The stairs are two blocks wide, with no ladder, and read as a way down. The room you arrive in is a story below, with its own lamp light and walls, and its doors line up with the stair room's lower door (no wall where the doorway should be, no bedrock plug). Mobs chase you down the stairs. The exit pad and the finale work on the lower story. Leaving the floor or dying cleans the whole volume, including the lower story.
- **Fail signs:** a bedrock or stone plug across the lower doorway; stairs you cannot climb back up or mobs that will not follow; a room hanging in the void with no floor; a dark or unlit stair; the floor never completing; leftover blocks one story down after the instance ends; a stair room on a Timber Adit or Mine Mouth floor; two stair rooms on a Mineshaft floor.
