## Intent versus experience gaps

- Home is meant to be a priced gamble (levels, chests, top-up versus depth bonus and omen head start). In play it is invisible; the go-home is driven by inventory.
- The kit top-up is meant to connect the economy to omen. It arrives silently into an inventory the player cannot see.
- Loot is meant to reward. At tier 1 it is mostly things with no immediate use, while the one universal need (wood) almost never drops.
