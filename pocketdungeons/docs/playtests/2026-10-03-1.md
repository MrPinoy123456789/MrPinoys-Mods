## Bugs and faults

### B1 (high, game): a failed run empties a new player's kit (PROBABLE CAUSE, not reproduced)
Evidence: before the fail the pack held flint and steel 64, blocks 3, food 8. After `run_failed` at 06:37:19 and the re-entry, `context` showed tools [], blocks 0, food 0 at HOME. The player: "I lost all my items and don't have my kit."
Cause from the code: `InventorySwap.snapshotOnEntry` (about line 840) takes the first interval snapshot with `computeIfAbsent` the moment the dungeon pack is swapped in. First entry was 06:14:16; `bag_chosen` was 06:16:09, so the snapshot holds an empty pack. Nothing refreshes it after `Bags.apply`. `Instances.failRunOmen` (line 1900) then restores that empty snapshot, and `kit_granted` is already true, so the one-time kit never returns.
Fix idea: refresh the member's interval snapshot after the bag is applied, or take the first snapshot at door commit.
Workaround used (with his consent): gave 3 tnt, 1 flint_and_steel, 8 bread by console (plain copies, not bag-tagged; bypasses the once-only rule).
