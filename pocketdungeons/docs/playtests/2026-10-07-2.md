## Findings

### The haul model, tested end to end

Every money path the model defines was exercised and journaled
correctly: floor pay into the haul (1 on underleveled floors, the dealt
step otherwise, zero emeralds), the home bank via the lever twice, the
finish bank (5 scrap banked, compass 1 to 2), the fifth-death fail bank
(4 of 8 kept, compass 0 to 1), the mid-trip compass lore (the Haul line
shows only in trip), the home lore (no Haul line), and the GO HOME board
(per-member haul, lives, unfinished, half-loss footer). Mechanically the
model is sound.

The model fails on legibility in the opposite direction to PD-162. Asked
to explain scrap in one sentence after a full session of pay, bank and
fail: "I can't tell yet, feels like it doesn't exist." The confusion is
gone; so is the presence. Nothing anchors scrap as a thing he earned:
the pay line is one aqua chat line among combat spam, the haul lives in
item lore he does not re-read, and the finish bank happens off-screen,
so the haul reads 0 when he next looks ("why is my haul bringing back
0?").

His own proposal, verbatim: "it should pay out on the lever when the
dungeon is finished too, or if the player quits/disconnects after
completing the dungeon but not pulling lever (failsafe)", and "pull the
lever is the payout ritual". A single legible cash-out moment may be
the anchor the model is missing.

### Design feedback (player verbatim or near)

- "pull the lever is the payout ritual": bank on the lever even after
  a finish; quit or disconnect after finishing is the failsafe case.
- Lives should scale with party size.
- Vaults usable once per player, not once per party.
- On a failed run, land the party at Home with a "Wasted" screen, not
  a boot out of the dungeon world (he had to /dungeon back in).
- Witch poison 30+ seconds too punishing; wants about 10.
- Copper chest loot thin versus the end-of-floor barrel.
- Spawner ejects are 50/50 key vs emeralds; skew toward keys.
- More mineshaft_seam-style rooms with visible ore; Rootworks palette
  nodes were invisible across three floors (0 mined of 19, 9, 25, 23).
- The floor-name and clear-title legibility items above.
