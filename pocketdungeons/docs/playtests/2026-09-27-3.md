## Harness notes

- **`wait` wakes itself.** Each `server wait --player <p>` starts by sending `dungeon lemon mode <p> llm` (pdserver.mjs:316), which logs a `lemon mode <p> llm` event; the next `wait` returns at once with only that line (seen at 19:35:49, 19:35:54, 19:36:43, 19:36:48). The 60 s refresh inside a wait does the same. Expected: `wait` ignores its own mode refresh. I used a scratchpad wrapper that loops `wait` and drops `lemon mode`, `lemon says` and `lemon asks` lines.
- **Undocumented event lines.** `lemon mode <p> llm|guide`, `lemon asks <p> ...` (my own ask echoed) and `lemon held <p> (fight) ...` appear but are not in table 3.1.
- **30 second prompted window.** A reply counts as prompted only while a question is pending or within 30 s of the player last addressing Lemon (`Lemon.deliver`, Lemon.java:301). Any lookup longer than that turns the answer into an unprompted line, which is held during a fight for up to two minutes (19:45, 20:38) with no sign to the player, who then thinks Lemon is ignoring them. The runbook's "within about 30 seconds" is exactly this window but does not say what happens past it. Suggest: keep a reply prompted until the agent answers, or add a `lemon think <p>` that hides Lemon and shows a "looking into it" line.
- **Vanish while looking.** The player asked for it (20:33). What worked: `lemon say` a short "checking" line, wait about 4 s for the bubble, then `lemon quiet` (which only hushes and despawns; it does not set the player's quiet flag, despite the runbook calling it the command to respect "quiet"). The runbook should say which quiet is which.
- **Mode resets on relog.** After the 19:40 relog, context showed lemon mode `guide` until the next `wait` refresh.
- **`server status` player list.** Printed `UP. There are 0 of a max of 20 players online: ` at 19:35:12, and a bare `UP. ` at 19:35:5x while the player was online (the RCON `list` reply came back empty).
- **`sync` then the join.** `sync` reported "Skipped 0 earlier event(s)" at about 19:35:3x, yet the first `wait` printed `19:35:23 join`. Harmless here (it let me greet), but `sync` did not skip an event logged before it ran.
- **Chat length.** Player messages over 256 characters are cut by Minecraft before Lemon sees them (19:52:11); ask the player to finish the thought.
- **Existing draft file.** `2026-09-27-2.md` already existed as a stub from an earlier Lemon run, so this session is `-3`. The runbook could say to continue a same-day draft whose session ended early, or to always start a new one.
