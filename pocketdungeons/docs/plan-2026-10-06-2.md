## A. Omen leaves the bank (D24)

Today `IntervalBanking.settle` returns a `band` that pays nothing (charts come
from scrap only; chests are a flat `baseRewardChests() + bonus`), but three
strings still talk about it.

- `IntervalBanking.Settlement`: drop `band`. `settle` loses `omenSum` and
  `penalty`. `LEAVE_PENALTY`, `WORST_BAND` and `band(...)` go.
- `RunLifecycle.settleOnce` and `settleInterval`: drop the penalty argument.
  A checkpoint exit (`RunLifecycle` ~995) and a grace expiry (~2019) settle
  exactly like the home lever; keep the `trigger` string for the journal.
- `IntervalBanking.bankedLine`: drop the "omen counts one band worse" opening
  and the trailing "The omen was ..." sentence.
- `PlaytestJournal.bank` and `PlayerContext` (~102): stop writing `band` on
  the bank event; keep the live omen in `context` (Lemon reads it in play).
- `OmenBar`, `OmenBarText` keep the band words for the bar, which is about
  danger now.
- Tests: `IntervalBankingTest` loses its band cases; one case asserts a
  checkpoint exit and a home lever bank the same charts.
- `DUNGEON_STRUCTURE_DESIGN.md` D16a: "Omen raises danger only; it never
  changes what a trip pays."

## B. Floor levels, scrap and emeralds (D26, D27, D28)

- `DungeonDef.Node` gains `level` (int, optional). When absent, a node's level
  is `baseLevel + layer - 1`.
- `DungeonDef.problems`: a node's level is at least the level of every node
  with an edge into it (difficulty never drops going forward); `baseLevel` is
  at least the dungeon's `unlockLevel` (C).

### The deal

- `TripDoors.dealFirst` and `dealNext`: every dungeon deals the `1, 2, 3`
  shuffle. The `RESOURCE ? 0 :` branches are gone with the kind (section G,
  this is D26). `TripDoorsTest` (~180 to 197) asserts the shuffle for the
  Mineshaft and Cow Pits now.
- `Keystone.fromDoor` and the plain door branch: the offer's level is
  `KeystoneMath.upgrade(nodeLevel, step, max)`, not `upgrade(compass, step,
  max)`. Add a pure `FloorLevels.of(DungeonDef, nodeId)` (base plus layer, or
  the node's `level`) with a unit test.
- Everything downstream already reads the offer's level (`record.layout`,
  `DifficultyProfile`, `AffixMath.seededFor`, the act's loot band), so mob
  strength, affixes and loot follow the floor, not the player.
- The Endless Mine has no nodes past its entry: its floor level is the layer's
  base (the act `baseLevel` of the layer's act: 1, 6, 13, 16) plus the floors
  into the layer, plus the step. Pure, in `EndlessMineRules`, tested.
- The experimental offer keeps its own `lootLevel` when set; otherwise the
  floor rule.

### Paying a floor

**Superseded in part by J1 (D32):** scrap is paid at the floor clear, not at a
bank, and nothing is lost going home. `FloorPay` and the over-level rule below
stand; read "at the bank" as "at the floor clear", and the over-level check
uses the member's highest chart level (J1).

- `IntervalBanking.effectiveScrap` goes. A pure `FloorPay.of(int step, int
  floorLevel, int memberLevel)` returns `scrap = step, emeralds = 0` when
  `memberLevel <= floorLevel`, else `scrap = 0, emeralds = step *
  overlevelEmeraldsPerScrap`.
- `PocketDungeonsConfig.overlevelEmeraldsPerScrap`, default 2, read and
  written like its neighbours.
- `IntervalBanking.settle` sums `FloorPay` over the cleared floors per member:
  charts from scrap as today (5 to a chart, remainder lost), emeralds summed.
- `RunLifecycle.settleInterval`: each member gets their emeralds into the pack
  at the bank, beside the chart message, and the journal `bank` event carries
  `emeralds`. Paid at the bank, not the floor clear, so a max-omen fail loses
  them with the scrap, the same stake.
- Party example for the tests: leader at compass 4 and a friend at 9 clear a
  level 5 floor dealt +2 (floor level 7): the leader banks 2 scrap, the friend
  4 emeralds.
- Tests: `FloorPayTest` (at, below, above the floor; config 2 and 3), an
  `IntervalBankingTest` case mixing paid and over-levelled floors.
- `DUNGEON_STRUCTURE_DESIGN.md`: D11 (the underlevel discount) and D12 amended;
  section 5 question about solo underlevelling marked resolved by D27.

## D. Completing an act (D29)

### The rule

Act N opens for a member when, for act N - 1:

1. every dungeon of the act that is not the Endless Mine (kind `dungeon` or
   `capstone`) is in the member's `dungeonsFinished`; and
2. the member's deepest Endless Mine floor has reached the bottom of the act's
   depth layer, for acts that have one: act 1 floor 5, act 2 floor 11, act 3
   floor 17, act 4 floor 23 (layer 4 has no bottom today; 23 gives it six
   floors like the others). Act 5 has no mine layer.

Pure, in `ActProgress.complete(int act, Set<String> finished, int deepestMineFloor,
Collection<DungeonDef> all)`, with a table driven test.

### Where it is checked

- `RunLifecycle.finishDungeon` (every finish, not only capstones) and the
  Endless Mine's floor clear: for each member present, re-evaluate the open
  acts and unlock any newly complete act through the same message, title and
  journal event `DungeonProgress.onCapstoneCleared` uses today. Rename it
  `DungeonProgress.onProgress`.
- Act 5's capstone still completes the campaign, but only when act 5 is
  complete by rule 1 (the End, Ender Archive and Herobrine all finished).
- Per member, as today: a friend carried through a capstone gets the capstone
  ticked, not the act, until their own list is complete.
- Never revoke. Saves already holding acts 2 or 3 (the keystone 15 migration)
  keep them.

### The Endless Mine has to open in act 1

Today it opens when act 2 opens (`EndlessMineRules.opensFor`, `OPENING_ACT`),
so it could never count toward act 1. Change `OPENING_ACT` to 1, with the
door 3 replacement gated on compass 3 (`endlessMineUnlockLevel`, config) so a
new player's first doors are dungeons. The layer seals by act stay as built.
**Owner check:** if the Mine should stay closed in act 1, rule 2 starts at act
2 instead; both are one constant.

### What the player sees

- The staging map gains an act checklist: each dungeon of the open act with a
  tick or its unlock level, the Mine's depth against its target, the capstone.
- A finish that completes the act shows the existing act title; a finish that
  does not names what is left: `Act 1: 2 dungeons and the Mine to floor 5 left.`
- `DUNGEON_STRUCTURE_DESIGN.md` D8, D13 and D16 amended.

## E. A kit free main path (D25)

Today the planner seeds solvability from the owner's bag
(`BagTags.seed(bagId, ...)` at `Instances` ~431 and ~1133), so a floor can need
the kit on its only way through. With kits granted once (D14) and no refills,
a Sapper who has spent their TNT can be dealt a floor they cannot pass. The
rule now:

- **The spine is solvable with nothing.** `Instances` (~431, ~1133) seed the
  planner with `BagTags.pilgrim()` plus party size (keep `MOB` for large
  parties) for the spine. A spine cell's `requires` must be met by the
  pilgrim seed plus what spine cells above it `provides`.
- **Spurs may need a tool.** A spur cell (off the entrance to staging path)
  may take a room whose `requires` names a tool tag (`explosive`, `water`,
  `lava`, `pearl`, and the other bag tags in `Bags.tagsFor`), whether or not
  the party carries it. These are the bonus rooms. `RoomSelector` already
  separates spine and spur (around line 1420, `onSpine`); widen the spur
  branch to the full tool tag set and stop filtering spurs by the bag.
- **Rubble only on spur doors.** `RoomSelector.pickRubbleEdges`: an edge is
  eligible only when it is not on the spine. Drop the `explosiveBelow` bag
  check; keep "at most one per floor" and the 35 percent.
- **Sealed lower stories are bonuses.** `pickSealedCells`: seal a two story
  cell whenever its lower story is not needed to cross the cell (the reward
  half of `blaze_cellar`, `slime_pit`, `sump`). Check each template: if the
  only way on runs through the lower story, that room never seals.
- **Explain the bonus.** A rubble plug's mining refusal already names what
  clears it; a tool room's sign or Lemon line says what opens it. No new UI.
- Tests: `GraphSolvabilityTest` plans every node of every shipped dungeon for
  a few hundred seeds with each of the 9 bags and asserts the spine never
  requires a tag outside the pilgrim seed and upstream `provides`; a case that
  a Sapper and a Pilgrim get spines with the same requirements; `PlanSelectorTest`
  rubble never on a spine edge.
- `docs/reference/ORDEALS.md` "Rubble doorways" and `DUNGEON_STRUCTURE_DESIGN.md`
  D14 amended.

## F. Gunpowder and sand (D25)

There is no gunpowder and no sand in any loot table today, and creepers spawn
only in `copper_works` and `creeper_kennel`. Gunpowder plus sand is TNT, which
opens the bonus rooms in E, so all three sources go in, each small:

- **Gunpowder in chests.** The supply tables (`loot_table/chests/supply_tier_*`
  and their themed copies) gain `minecraft:gunpowder` 1 to 3, weight about
  half of bones. Generate through `tools/gen_themed_content.py`, never by hand.
- **More creepers.** One creeper entry at low weight in the act 1 and act 2
  `undead` spawner family (`TrialContent.GENERIC_FAMILIES`, from the
  2026-10-02 batch) and in the Mineshaft roster. Not in dark rooms' first spawn wave (a
  creeper in the dark is a surprise death, check after the test).
- **Sand as a resource node.** `minecraft:sand` in the Mineshaft and Lush Caves
  `nodePalette`, `minecraft:red_sand` in Basalt Foundry's. Sand is not a
  structural block (`DungeonDef.STRUCTURAL_BLOCKS`), so it validates. Add sand
  pockets to the hidden ore (plan 2026-10-06-1 item 5b): `blocks` gains sand at
  a weight like gold.
- Live check: a TNT crafted from dungeon gunpowder and sand opens a rubble spur.

## J. The simplification pass (D32 to D38)

Goal: a player tracks one progress bar, one spending currency (emeralds) and
one risk meter (lives). Every item below removes a rule or a screen line.

### J1. Scrap is experience, charts are levels (D32, D33)

- Every member keeps `scrap` (points) and `highestCharts` (permanent) on
  `DungeonLog.Entry`. The chart level is `floor(scrap / SCRAP_PER_CHART)`;
  the compass item shows it, and the level bar shows the progress to the next
  chart, the way the XP bar does. Flat 5 scrap a chart for the next test; a
  curve is a later knob (`scrapPerChart(level)`, pure).
- **Earned at the clear.** `RunLifecycle` floor completion adds each
  member's `FloorPay` scrap (or over-level emeralds) on the spot, with a line
  and a sound. The go-home "bank" stops converting anything:
  `IntervalBanking.settle` shrinks to the journal row, and the GO HOME board
  (plan 2026-10-06-1 item 4) loses its scrap and chart lines. It reads what
  the dungeon left unclaimed instead: `Unfinished: vault, page`.
- **Spent at the door.** A side edge's cost is scrap (D33): the authored
  `cost` in each `dungeon/*.json` doubles (1 shard becomes 2 scrap, 2 becomes
  4), a knob for the test. `RunLifecycle.commitDoor` takes the cost from the
  member who pulls the lever, as shards are taken today, and refuses with
  `Not enough scrap: 4 needed, 3 carried.`
- **Unlocks are permanent.** `highestCharts` rises whenever the level does
  and never falls. Every gate reads it: dungeon `unlockLevel` (C), the act
  checklist's level lines, and the over-level emerald rule (B), so spending
  scrap never re-opens cheap scrap on low floors.
- **Echo shards retire as currency.** `Fuel` (the shard bank) goes. Its
  income becomes emeralds: the dungeon finish pays `finishEmeralds` (8), an
  Ordeal resolution pays 2 emeralds at the existing 0.25 chance, the Lemon
  archive bonus pays 8. Echo shards stay as ordinary rare chest loot (they
  still craft a recovery compass). Migration: each banked shard becomes 2
  scrap on first login, with one line saying so.
- Tests: pure `ScrapMath` (level from points, permanent high, spend refusal,
  migration), `FloorPayTest` reads `highestCharts`.

### J2. Failure costs only the stake (D34)

- `Instances.failRunOmen`: no `restoreIntervalSnapshot`, no
  `RunStorage.rollBackToInterval`. Everyone is sent to the Doors' home side
  with what they carry. Scrap spent on doors is already gone; the dungeon's
  finish (vault, page, `finishEmeralds`, act checklist tick) is not paid.
- Remove `IntervalState.inventorySnapshot` and its capture
  (`InventorySwap.snapshotOnEntry`); PD-121 and its first-snapshot timing go
  with it.
- The fail line: `The dungeon claims you. You keep what you carry.`
- Tests: the failure gametest asserts the pack is unchanged and no finish
  reward was paid.

### J3. Only death raises omen; omen is lives (D35)

Omen stays 0 to 4 per trip, and a death at 4 still ends the dungeon. Death is
now the only thing that raises it. The board and bar show it as lives,
`5 - omen`: a party starts at `Lives 5`, each death takes one, and the death
at `Lives 1` ends the dungeon (the same five deaths as today's rule).

| Trigger today | Becomes |
|---|---|
| Dwell in an unsolved room (`OmenSources` ~249) | After the same 60 s grace, a lootless wave (`Instances.spawnOmenWave`) every 90 s while you stay. |
| Sculk sensor pulse (~190) | A small wave at the sensor. In the Ancient City every pulse counts toward the Warden: the fourth pulse on the final floor wakes it (it now counts pulses, not omen). |
| Shriek (~197) | Darkness on everyone in the cell plus a wave. |
| The Ominous Bargain (~209) | The bargain's price is a harder floor: `+2` floor level for the rest of the floor instead of omen. |
| Silence, every 3 consumables (~285) | A wave instead of omen. |
| Zone depth head start (`ZoneRules.baseOmen`) | Removed (trips are one dungeon now). |
| Capstone start omen (`capstoneStartOmen`) | Becomes a `+2` level on capstone final floors. |
| Fountain boon "omen relief" | "A life back": omen minus 1, never below 0. |

- Danger scaling per omen (`omenDangerScalePerOmen` 0.15) stays: each death
  makes the rest of the dungeon harder, the "bad omen" the death line names.
- `OmenBar` / `OmenBarText`: the bar title becomes `Spawners 2/3 | Lives 3`;
  the rise cue on a death says `A bad omen. 2 lives left.`
- Tests: `OmenSourcesTest` (each trigger spawns, none adds omen),

### J5a. The home vendor (owner, 2026-10-06)

One vendor at home, the librarian a lectern brings. No blacksmith. The shop
is a 3 by 3 grid, a category per row and a tier per column, plus Mending and
the buy list. Buying a cell rolls one random item of that category and tier,
the way the Gamble drew from `gear/<slot>_<tier>` (that draw code moves here).

| Row | What one purchase rolls | Enchants |
|---|---|---|
| Armour | helmet, chestplate, leggings or boots | armour enchants |
| Weapon | sword, bow, crossbow or axe | weapon enchants (the axe gets sharpness and the like) |
| Tool | axe, shovel or pickaxe | tool enchants (the axe gets efficiency and the like) |

| Tier | Materials | Armour | Weapon | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | leather or copper, stone or copper, wood or stone tools | 6 | 9 | 6 |
| II | iron and chainmail | 12 | 18 | 12 |
| III | diamond | 18 | 27 | 18 |

- Prices are the Gamble's (6 emeralds a tier, weapons 1.5 times), as a start.
  Netherite is never sold: it stays a dungeon reward (Act 4 and on).
- A tier is offered once the player's act loot band reaches it (D10): Act 1
  sees I and II, Act 2 adds III. A locked cell shows a gray pane with
  `Opens in Act 2`.
- **Mending:** one enchanted book with Mending for **64 emeralds**, any number
  of times. Replaces `lockInEmeralds` (32) and the Lock In action. Applied at
  an anvil, the vanilla way.
- **Buy list:** the bottom row, half the dungeon merchants' rate (J4).
- Every item sold carries the dungeon durability cap (PD-146) and the tier
  tag, like loot.
- **Loot tables:** `gear/armour_<tier>` (merging the helmet, chestplate,
  leggings and boots tables per tier), `gear/weapon_<tier>` (exists; check
  each has sword, bow, crossbow and axe, and drop the trident from tier 3),
  new `gear/tool_<tier>`. Enchant functions as the existing gear tables use,
  with a tool enchant set for tools. The slot tables stay for chests and
  vaults.
- Layout, a 6 row chest: rows 1 to 3 the grid (columns 3 to 5, a category
  label item in column 2), row 4 Mending, rows 5 and 6 the buy list. Same
  click rules as the PD-159 one-row shop (`StoreNPC.openShop`): click buys,
  red price when short, no item can be taken out.
- Tests: a pure `HomeVendor` price and tier gate test; a gametest that buys
  one cell of each row and checks the item's category, tier and durability
  cap; Mending takes 64 and delivers the book.

### J8. Door crafting with the Cube (design only, built after the test)

The owner's intent: the Herobrine Cube crafts a door to fit what the player
needs, the way Path of Exile maps are rolled and modified. A draft to review:

- **The door item.** The Cube takes a **base**: the diary page of a dungeon
  the player has finished (so only known places can be crafted), and makes a
  **sealed map** (a vanilla `filled_map` with a name and lore) for that
  dungeon.
- **Modifiers.** Catalyst items added in the Cube add modifiers, one each,
  at most three: the 12 existing recipes become them (`flooded`, `infested`,
  `feral`, `ominous`, `deep_dark`, `blaze_bias`, `bazaar_bias`, `store`,
  `slime_guarantee`, `path_extension`, `bounded_supply`, `compass`). Each
  modifier raises the floor levels by 1 and the scrap paid by 1 a floor.
- **Cost.** Crafting costs scrap: 2 plus 2 a modifier. A failed crafted
  dungeon loses the map and its cost (the D34 stake, nothing else).
- **Use.** At the Doors, using the map on the first staging room replaces
  door 3 with the crafted dungeon; the door board lists its modifiers in
  magenta like affixes. The Endless Mine moves to door 2 that trip.
- **Open:** whether maps drop as loot too (PoE style), whether a map can be
  re-rolled, and whether crafted dungeons count for the act checklist (D29).
