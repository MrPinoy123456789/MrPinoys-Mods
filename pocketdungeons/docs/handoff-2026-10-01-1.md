Fix a batch of Pocket Dungeons issues from the 2026-10-01 live playtest. The
mod is at `pocketdungeons/` (Java, Fabric, Minecraft 26.2). Session evidence is
`docs/playtests/2026-10-01-2.md`; bug details are `docs/reference/BUGS.md`

## Bugs to fix

1. **PD-98 (High): `kennel_crossing` stamps with no spawner.** Player stood in
   the room and nothing spawned; the log shows `Situation encounter cell at
   160, 64, 512 has no spawner anchor; no trial spawner placed`
   (`TrialContent.applyEncounter`, around line 323). `SituationSpecs.
   kennelCrossing` declares `.spawner(new BlockPos(8, 1, 8))` but no `.spawns()`
   anchor list, and the NBT has no authored trial spawner, so both lookup paths
   fail and the method returns early, before `clearClassicSpawners` runs.
   Audit `sensor_gallery` and `blaze_cellar` for the same defect. Fix by
   falling back to the spec's `.spawner()` position or by adding authored trial
   spawners to the NBTs; add a gametest that stamps each situation combat room
   and asserts a configured trial spawner exists. Also decide whether the
   intended design is wolves spawning inside the fenced pen (then escaping or
   being released); the player guessed they may spawn outside and disperse.

2. **PD-99 (Medium): `rotation_lock` may be unsolvable.** Player: "the
   comparator was backwards and wasn't working" (`MechanismSpecs.java` around
   line 343). Verify the comparator, redstone block and dust survive all four
   room rotations; add a gametest that stamps each rotation, sets the frame to
   rotation 8, and asserts the door opens.

3. **PD-100 (Low): the SENSOR omen line vanishes too fast to read.** "The sculk
   counts your steps; the next fight will be harder." (`OmenBarText.java:116`)
   disappeared before the player finished reading. Extend its display time or
   keep it up while sensors keep pulsing.

4. **PD-101 (Medium): the Salvage Bench deposits the held item on open.**
   Player expects a plain right-click to only open the station
   (`SalvageStation.java`, the PD-96 fix site). Change the open path so it does
   not move the held stack; depositing should be a deliberate action inside the
   screen. Keep the sneak-for-vanilla-grindstone behavior.

5. **PD-102 (High): `Commit stamp failed behind the staging room`.** Logged at
   07:51 (`Instances.java` around line 1410), followed by the player
   disconnecting in PREVIEW at 08:13. Find the thrown exception in
   `run/logs/latest.log` (or the rotated log for that window) and fix the
   cause; check what state a preview is left in if the player disconnects
   mid-commit.

6. **PD-103 (Medium): blacksmiths multiply via the staging room.** A
   blacksmith that wanders out the door into the staging room leaves the
   sweep's 12-block scan box (`BlacksmithNPC.findAllBlacksmiths`, ~line 190),
   so `sweep` sees none and spawns a replacement while the escapee is never
   found, anchored, or discarded. Fix by widening the search to cover the
   staging area (or the whole instance cell block), blocking the doorway
   crossing, or freezing AI near it.

## Economy and design changes to weigh (player asks, not all bugs)

- **Lapis is effectively absent:** only `supply_tier_2`, weight 2, count 1-2.
  Player wants lapis and enchanting to arrive together. Raise weight or add
  lapis to more pools.
- **Enchanting table should be crafted, not looted:** it is currently a loot
  drop; he got one without remembering where. Consider removing it from loot.
- **Netherite too early:** he got netherite leggings at keystone ~9-10 and
  asked for gating. Check which loot pools emit netherite and at what tier.
- **Mending is too common:** "a lot of mending enchanted gear" plus "mending
  makes gear last forever", which sits oddly next to loot gear dying fast
  (third durability report). **Owner direction (2026-10-01):** make Mending an
  expensive operation that "locks in" a weapon, tool or armour piece, rather
  than a common random enchant on loot. Likely a station action (salvage
  bench or anvil-adjacent) paid in emeralds, which also answers the
  emerald-sink ask. Open detail for the owner: whether a locked item is
  exempt from salvage and reroll. Still investigate PD-89 alongside: what
  durability do dropped gear items spawn with?
- **Totem of undying needs a use:** player proposes an omen-reducing mechanic
  (matches the consumable-omen idea from 2026-09-30-2). Owner decision needed.
- **Blacksmith table needs a discovery hint** (same class as PD-96).
- **Sand in foundry/brewing pools:** requested alongside the bottles (L8).
- **Mining Efficiency enchant is dead** in a dungeon where blocks barely
  matter; consider trimming it from enchant pools.
- **Dungeon-only ender chest:** player proposal to counter the M46 denial;
  a stash usable only inside the dungeon. Owner decision; it changes what the
  bag means.
- **HUD via text display** (like the floor-clear title) as a persistent HUD.
  Feature idea, note for the owner.
- **Stacked chest decor:** an unintentionally stacked chest read as a cool
  anomaly to the player. If a chest-on-chest placement in some room was not
  intended, decide whether to keep it deliberately.
