# Roguelite Content Brainstorm

A scratchpad of Minecraft-native perks, relics, tier themes, and meta
progression ideas for Pocket Dungeons. Nothing here is specced or
committed; it is the raw material for a future milestone. Generated
2026-08-31.

The goal: every entry should read as an extension of vanilla Minecraft,
not a foreign system bolted on. Piglins enter at the nether tier, the
Warden tier is a stealth heist, relics are recognizable vanilla objects
reframed, and so on.

---

## Run Perks

Pick 1 of 3 at door milestones, roguelite style.

- **Second Wind**: totem-style cheat death, once per run
- **Lucky Miner**: ores in dungeon rooms drop double
- **Piglin's Favor**: emeralds occasionally drop as gold, tradeable at better rates
- **Sprinting Start**: permanent Speed I inside the dungeon
- **Feather Bones**: no fall damage, slow fall on jumps over 3 blocks
- **Hearty Stew**: food heals instantly like suspicious stew
- **Arrow Recycler**: 50% chance arrows return to inventory
- **Ender Pockets**: portable ender chest access mid-run
- **Beacon Heart**: standing still 3s grants Regeneration
- **Thorned Hide**: reflect a portion of melee damage
- **Soul Speed Initiate**: killing a mob grants brief speed burst

---

## Relics / Artifacts

Rare, persist for the whole run, probably 1 slot.

- **Cracked Nether Star**: aura giving nearby party members Resistance I
- **Dragon's Breath Vial**: crits leave a lingering damage cloud
- **Warden's Earbone**: see mobs through walls when sneaking (glowing)
- **Evoker's Grimoire**: chance to summon a friendly vex on kill
- **Blaze Core**: fire immunity, but water hurts you
- **Bell of the Village**: ring to reveal the room layout / minimap ping
- **Compass of the Lost**: points to the vault or exit door
- **Wither Rose Crown**: +damage, but slow health drain (risk relic)

## Curses / Mutations

Accepted for reward, roguelite trade-offs.

- **Glass Cannon**: half hearts, double damage
- **Phantom Marked**: phantoms spawn periodically, but drop membranes and XP
- **Hoarder's Burden**: inventory locked at 9 slots, +50% loot quality
- **Silence**: no potions this run, +1 perk choice
- **Creeper Magnetism**: more creepers, but they drop music discs / TNT

## Room / Floor Modifiers

M+ affix flavor.

- **Thundering**: periodic skeleton horde with lightning telegraphs
- **Overgrown**: vines and moss slow you; mobs unaffected
- **Souls of the Deep**: soul sand floors, sculk sensors trigger reinforcements
- **Bountiful**: extra trial spawner, extra vault key chance
- **Eclipse**: darkness effect pulses, mobs get Invisibility flickers
- **Molten**: random floor blocks turn to magma; drops smelted loot

## Boons

Shrine-style, one-shot pickups in rooms.

- **Anvil Blessing**: free repair + one random enchant level
- **Brewing Altar**: choose 1 of 3 potion effects for the floor
- **Sacrificial Campfire**: burn an item for hearts or XP
- **Conduit Fragment**: next water room grants conduit power

## Items / Consumables

Dungeon-only.

- **Recall Scroll** (echo shard craft): return to your room, keep loot
- **Skeleton Key**: opens any vault once
- **Bottled Thunder**: throwable lightning strike
- **Shepherd's Horn**: taunt all mobs in the room to one spot
- **Torch Bundle**: auto-places torches as you walk (anti-spawn)

## Traits

Permanent keystone unlocks, meta progression.

- **Cartographer**: rooms reveal on the tracker screen as explored
- **Bargainer**: blacksmith NPC prices reduced per keystone tier
- **Deep Pockets**: +1 relic slot at high keystone level
- **Veteran's Instinct**: elite mobs get a visible glow outline

## Tier Themes

Tier ladder, each themed as a natural escalation of vanilla progression.
Escalation levers across tiers: mob armor tier matches the theme (wood
tier mobs unarmored, nether tier in gold, warden tier in netherite
scraps), trap density rises, light level falls, and loot tables shift one
gear tier ahead of what the player likely has.

### T1 - Wood (Overworld Surface)

- **Palette**: oak/birch planks, dirt, leaves, cobwebs, campfires
- **Mobs**: zombies, spiders, the odd skeleton; pillager scouts as elites
- **Hazards**: cobweb tunnels, leaf floors that drop you a level
- **Loot bias**: food, arrows, leather, basic tools
- **Signature room**: abandoned lumber camp with a chest cart
- **Miniboss**: Pillager Captain (drops Bad Omen bottle as a curse-currency)

### T2 - Stone (Caves)

- **Palette**: cobble, deepslate patches, dripstone, glow lichen, rails
- **Mobs**: skeletons, creepers, cave spiders, silverfish in infested blocks
- **Hazards**: pointed dripstone drops, gravel collapses, ravine gaps
- **Loot bias**: coal, iron nuggets, stone-tier gear, minecart chests
- **Signature room**: mineshaft junction, spawner in a cage of rails
- **Miniboss**: Silverfish Broodmother (block-infesting swarm fight)

### T3 - Iron (Stronghold / Dungeon proper)

- **Palette**: stone bricks (cracked/mossy), iron bars, chains, lanterns
- **Mobs**: armored zombies/skeletons, vindicators, iron-golem-frame turrets
- **Hazards**: dispenser arrow traps, piston crushers, tripwires
- **Loot bias**: iron gear, shields, anvils, enchanted books (low tier)
- **Signature room**: library with hidden lever passage
- **Miniboss**: Rusted Golem (corrupted iron golem, slow but arena-denying slams)

### T4 - Diamond (Deep Dark fringe / Lush-Amethyst caves)

- **Palette**: amethyst clusters, calcite, smooth basalt, sculk veins creeping in
- **Mobs**: witches, breezes, first sculk-buffed variants (faster, quiet)
- **Hazards**: sculk sensors wired to spawners, amethyst shard floors (noise!)
- **Loot bias**: diamonds, better enchants, first relic-tier drops
- **Signature room**: geode vault, key behind a silence challenge (no sprinting)
- **Miniboss**: Coven Matron (witch with vex summons and lingering clouds)

### T5 - Nether

- **Palette**: netherrack, blackstone, crimson/warped wood, basalt, lava falls
- **Mobs**: **piglins introduced here**: brutes as elites, snipers with crossbows; blazes, magma cubes, hoglins
- **Faction mechanic**: wear gold to walk past piglin patrol rooms; barter altar takes gold for perks
- **Hazards**: lava lakes, ghast bombardment corridors, soul sand slow zones
- **Loot bias**: gold gear, fire resist potions, netherite scrap (rare)
- **Signature room**: bastion treasure vault guarded by a brute pack
- **Miniboss**: Piglin Warchief (gold-armored brute, enrages if you loot mid-fight)

### T6 - End

- **Palette**: end stone, purpur, obsidian, chorus plants, end rods, void gaps
- **Mobs**: endermen, shulkers, endermites, phantoms over the void
- **Hazards**: void bridges, shulker levitation into drops, teleport-swapped rooms
- **Mechanic**: chorus fruit as tactical teleport consumable; rooms may rearrange
- **Loot bias**: ender pearls, elytra fragments, shulker shells, high enchants
- **Signature room**: end ship boarding fight
- **Miniboss**: Void Herald (enderman shaman that teleports the *player*)

### T7 - Warden (Deep Dark)

- **Palette**: full sculk, reinforced deepslate, soul lanterns, ancient city bones
- **Mobs**: few but lethal; sculk-infested versions of every prior tier's mobs
- **Core mechanic**: stealth tier: sensors and shriekers everywhere, darkness pulses, sound = death; wool paths as safe routes
- **Hazards**: shrieker warning levels persist across rooms; Warden stalks at max
- **Loot bias**: echo shards, swift sneak, best relics, keystone ascension item
- **Signature room**: ancient city center, loot pile directly under a shrieker
- **Boss**: the Warden itself, but the real win condition is the heist: grab and escape

### T8+ - Ascended / Prestige (optional cap)

- Mixed-theme "corrupted" remixes of earlier tiers with stacked affixes
- Herobrine-flavored anomalies (ties into the Herobrine Cube station)
- Cosmetic + leaderboard rewards rather than power

## Meta Progression Expansions

Ways to deepen the existing keystone system. Natural first picks given
what is already built: keystone sockets, station upgrade tiers, and
bounty streaks, since they extend systems that already exist (keystone,
room stations, M34 bounties) rather than requiring new infrastructure.

### Keystone Extensions

- **Keystone sockets**: slot gems/relics into the keystone for permanent passive bonuses; higher tiers unlock more sockets
- **Keystone specializations**: at milestone levels pick a path (Raider / Explorer / Merchant) that biases perk pools and loot tables
- **Prestige / reforging**: reset keystone level for a permanent account-wide bonus and a cosmetic keystone skin
- **Keystone charge**: overleveling banks charges spendable on a guaranteed relic or a run mulligan

### Unlock Tracks

- **Perk codex**: perks must be found once in a run before they enter the pick pool; codex completion gives rewards
- **Relic gallery**: discovered relics display in the player's room; full sets grant a small permanent buff
- **Theme unlocks**: beating a tier's boss X times unlocks its theme as a selectable run modifier with bonus loot
- **Starting loadouts**: unlock alternate starting kits (archer, alchemist, shield-bearer) via achievements

### Player Room Growth

- **Room expansions**: earn extra room wings; each wing hosts a new station
- **Station upgrade tiers**: blacksmith stock quality, reroll cost, cube power library all upgradeable with dungeon currency
- **Trophy mounts**: boss heads / mob trophies with tiny passive auras when displayed
- **Garden / farm plot**: grows dungeon-only consumables between runs (time-gated)
- **Pet stable**: rescued mobs (wolf, cat, allay) become room pets; allay fetches loot in runs once bonded

### Economy and Currency Layers

- **Echo shards as meta-currency**: spend on permanent unlocks, distinct from in-run emeralds
- **Weekly bounty streaks**: consecutive completed bounty weeks escalate rewards (ties into the M34 tracker)
- **Bad Omen bank**: store curse levels to spend on high-risk high-reward ominous runs
- **Barter reputation**: piglin/villager faction rep persists, unlocking better trade tables per tier

### Mastery and Challenge Systems

- **Weapon masteries**: kills with each weapon class rank up small permanent bonuses (sword crit, bow draw speed)
- **Tier mastery stars**: per-tier challenges (no damage, no torches, speedrun) each granting a star; stars gate a prestige vendor
- **Affix resistance**: repeatedly clearing a given affix slowly unlocks a counter-perk for it
- **Nemesis mobs**: an elite that kills you returns next run named and buffed; killing it drops bonus loot

### Account / Social

- **Party bonds**: repeated runs with the same party build a bond level with shared perks
- **Guild board**: server-wide weekly challenge with a shared reward pool
- **Leaderboards + seasonal resets**: seasonal keystone ladders with cosmetic-only season rewards
- **Legacy chest**: on prestige, send one item forward to your next cycle

### Knowledge as Progression

- **Diary/lore collection**: found diaries (already in the lore pass) unlock map hints, secret room spawn chances
- **Bestiary**: kill counts reveal mob weaknesses as literal in-game tooltips, then small damage bonuses
- **Cartography table meta**: mapping enough rooms of a tier reveals its layout archetypes on the tracker screen
