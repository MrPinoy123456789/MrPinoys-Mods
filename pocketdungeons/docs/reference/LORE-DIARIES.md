# Alex's Diaries

*Found out of order. Written over the course of Alex's expedition.*

---

## Entry 1 — The Gift

Page 1

He gave me his compass before he left.

His own.

I asked where he was going.

He said, "Down."

I asked why.

He wouldn't say.

It has been spinning for eleven days.

---

**Page 2**

It stopped this morning.

It isn't pointing north.

It is pointing somewhere I have never been.

I'm following it.

---

## Entry 2 — The Wrong Rooms

**Page 1**

The stronghold is wrong.

Not damaged. Wrong.

The corridors connect to rooms that shouldn't exist, and rooms lead back to themselves.

I marked one with a torch.

When I came back, the torch was on the other side of the room.

---

**Page 2**

I found my footprints.

They were ahead of me.

I checked the boots.

Mine.

I followed them.

They ended where I was standing.

---

**Page 3**

There is something underneath the stone.

It isn't a mob.

I know what mobs sound like.

This sounds like something trying to remember how to breathe.

---

## Entry 3 — The Whole Room

**Page 1**

I found one room that was right.

Every block where it belonged.

Every torch properly placed.

Someone remembered it perfectly.

I think it was his.

---

**Page 2**

The compass points beneath it.

I have been sitting here since yesterday.

I keep sharpening my pick.

It doesn't need sharpening.

---

**Page 3**

I found him.

There wasn't a body.

The room was him.

He knew me.

I wish he hadn't.

---

**Page 4**

He asked me to stop him.

I understand now.

---

## Entry 4 — The Seal

**Page 1**

I sealed the chamber.

He is still there.

The rooms are still changing.

So the two things are separate.

I don't know if that's better.

---

**Page 2**

I came back up today.

The grass looked strange.

I had forgotten that the sky was so large.

---

**Page 3**

The compass still points down.

I thought it was leading me to him.

I think it is leading me back to the work.

---

## Entry 5 — The Other

**Page 1**

Someone has been here.

A torch I didn't place.

A furnace.

A chest.

Cobblestone and bread.

Someone is making a base.

---

**Page 2**

They are following my route.

Not approximately.

Exactly.

I found their footprints beside mine.

---

**Page 3**

They have a compass.

I never saw them make one.

---

**Page 4**

I left my first journal where they will find it.

I wonder what they will think when they read it.

---

## Entry 6 — The Becoming

**Page 1**

My footprints are in the stone now.

I remember his looking like that.

I was frightened then.

---

**Page 2**

The place remembers everything that enters it.

Rooms.

Blocks.

People.

I have been coming back for a long time.

There is more of me down here every time.

---

**Page 3**

I found a room today that looked like my childhood home.

It was almost perfect.

The window was on the wrong wall.

---

**Page 4**

I think I know what happened to him.

I think I know what is happening to me.

I'm not sure which is worse.

---

## Entry 7 — The Work

**Page 1**

You have been here before.

I know because I found your torch.

You placed it beside the door.

Good habit.

---

**Page 2**

Leave your books behind.

Someone will find them.

They may need to know what happened here.

Or they may simply want to know where the good loot is.

Either is fine.

---

**Page 3**

I don't check the seal anymore.

I can feel when it needs me.

---

**Page 4**

The compass has stopped moving.

Mine did too.

So did his.

---

**Page 5**

You will come back.

Everyone does.

I used to wonder why.

I don't anymore.
