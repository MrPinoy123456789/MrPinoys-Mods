# Pocket Dungeons audit, round 2 (2026-09-26)

Static audit of the working tree as it stood on 2026-09-26, uncommitted edits
included: 151 main source files, 43,663 lines (34% comments), 48 unit tests, 15
gametest classes. Every item marked **Confirmed** was traced through the code
path end to end. Items marked **Needs a live check** are sound on the code but
depend on client behaviour I cannot run. Nothing in the source tree was changed.

The first audit (`docs/AUDIT.md`, 48 findings) is fully closed. This one is
different in kind: most of what is wrong now is not an isolated defect but the
cost of 78 milestones layered onto one mutable record. The loop was rebuilt
three times (U8 timer runs, M55 safe/staging split, M65 floor loop), and each
rebuild left the previous one's state, config and copy running alongside it.

## Headline

1. **Rewards get worse the longer a player stays in.** Floor omens are never
   reset after a safe-room visit, so every interval's omen carries into the
   next and the band slides from 3 chests and +1 level to 1 chest and +0.
2. **In a party, only the first player onto the exit pad gets credit.** The
   phase flips on the first touch, and everyone after is silently refused.
3. **Disconnecting between floors throws away the whole interval**, and the
   player is told "Your dungeon was closed while you were away" about a
   dungeon that is still open.
4. **The one pressure system that matters is invisible.** Omen decides chest
   count and level-up, and no screen, dialog or chat line ever shows it. The
   clock that does show (`Time: 5:00` on the door screen) no longer costs
   anything.
5. **Saving a room destroys paintings, pets and dropped items** in the
   player's own home, every time it saves.

The complexity recommendations in section 5 would remove the conditions that
produced items 1 to 3, not just patch them.

## 1. Bugs

### B1. Floor omens accumulate across safe visits *(High, Confirmed)*

`advanceFloor` appends each floor's omen to `record.floorOmens` and keys the
finish table off the sum (`RunLifecycle.java:1192-1196`). The only place that
list is cleared is `resetToLobby`, the `/dungeon quit` path
(`Instances.java:1516`). The two homecoming resets, `returnToSafe`
(`RunLifecycle.java:1556-1567`) and `fallbackTeleportHomecoming`
(`RunLifecycle.java:1670-1695`), reset `omen` but not `floorOmens`, and
`clearPreviousRunState` does not touch it either.

Scenario, default `floorsPerSafeVisit = 3` (bands: sum 0 to 3 low, 4 to 9 mid,
10+ high): a player who takes one point of omen per floor gets band 0 on visit
1 (sum 3), band 1 on visits 2 and 3 (sums 6, 9), and band 2 from visit 4 on
(sum 12): one chest, no keystone level. It resets only when they leave the
dungeon entirely and the lobby is purged, so it punishes exactly the players
who play longest. Dwell omen (one point per 90 s after 60 s in an unsolved
cell) makes a non-zero floor the normal case.

Fix: clear `floorOmens` in the homecoming reset. Better: section 5.1, which
makes per-interval state a fresh object so no reset list can miss a field.

### B2. Party members after the first get no floor credit *(High, Confirmed)*

`onTick` calls `completeRun` for each member who steps onto the pad
(`Instances.java:2157`). `completeRun` opens with
`RunSession.require(record, ACTIVE)` (`RunLifecycle.java:1044`), and the first
completion calls `advanceFloor`, which transitions to `FLOOR_CLEARED`
(`RunLifecycle.java:1268`). Every later member is refused with a WARN log line
and no message.

Lost for them: `recordCompletion` (runs completed, best path), `recordTheme`,
the guided task `COMPLETE_RUN`, the completion chime, and the run record behind
`/dungeon memento`, which is gated on `record.completed.contains(member)`
(`RunLifecycle.java:1387`). That includes the **owner** whenever a party member
reaches the pad first. Keystone level-ups are unaffected because
`settleSafeVisit` loops over all members.

Fix: record completion for any member who touches the pad while the floor's
completion is `ACTIVE` or already `FLOOR_CLEARED` for this floor; only the
first touch should advance the floor.

### B3. Leaving between floors forfeits the interval *(High, Confirmed)*

`isReenterable` requires `record.completed.isEmpty()`
(`RunLifecycle.java:281-284`), and `completed` stays non-empty for the whole
`FLOOR_CLEARED` phase. So an owner who disconnects (or runs `/dungeon exit`)
in a staging room between floors:

1. is kicked to their overworld return point on rejoin, with "Your dungeon was
   closed while you were away" (`Instances.java:231-246`, `1937`), although the
   instance is still live;
2. cannot step back in: `/dungeon` skips free re-entry, and `enter()` then
   retires the instance as "new run started" (`RunLifecycle.java:347-353`);
3. loses every floor since the last safe visit with no settlement at all.

The same thing happens silently after `rewardRoomGraceSeconds` (600 s) with
nobody active in the instance, because the grace window arms on
`!record.completed.isEmpty()` (`Instances.java:2044`), a rule from the
single-run era that now fires at every checkpoint.

Even mid-floor (`ACTIVE`, reenterable), the rejoin path teleports the player
out with the same wrong message and makes them type `/dungeon` to get back.

Fix: on JOIN, if the player owns a reenterable instance, put them back into
it. Make `FLOOR_CLEARED` reenterable. Arm the grace window only on a truly
finished run. Section 5.2 removes the special case entirely.

### B4. Only the final floor's door choice banks *(Medium, Confirmed; balance)*

`settleSafeVisit` banks `offers[record.chosenStep - 1]` for each member
(`RunLifecycle.java:1346-1351`), and `chosenStep` is whatever door opened the
*last* floor. Doors are +1 (free), +2 (ominous) and +3
(`Keystone.java:157-161`). Taking the free door twice and a Greater door once
banks +3, the same as three Greater doors, for a third of the fuel and a
fraction of the risk. Nothing on screen explains the rule.

Fix: bank per floor (sum or minimum of the steps actually cleared), or show
the rule on the door screen. See section 4.

### B5. Standing on a lodestone in your own room ejects you *(Medium, Confirmed; needs a one minute live check)*

In `HOME` the record carries `lobbyLayout`, whose keystone level is 0
(`Instances.java:846`), so `isKeystoneRun()` is false
(`InstanceRecord.java:516`). `isOnExitPad` accepts any lodestone inside the
layout bounds, the room cell or the staging cell (`Instances.java:2281-2293`).
The non-keystone branch then calls `RunLifecycle.exit(player, EXIT_PAD)`
(`Instances.java:2160-2165`). Nothing prevents a player from placing a
lodestone as decoration (no protection rule references it). Step on it at home
and you are thrown to the overworld.

Fix: drop the stand-on pad branch for anything except the terminal pad of an
active floor.

### B6. Room saves destroy paintings, pets and loose items *(Medium, Confirmed)*

`RoomStore.capture` discards every entity in the cell except players, item
frames and armour stands before capturing (`RoomStore.java:116-122`).
Paintings, tamed pets (including the wolf the `TAME_WOLF` task asks for),
display entities, leash knots, minecarts and dropped items are deleted. Saves
run on commit (twice), on every owner exit, on disconnect and on purge, so a
decorated room loses them the first time the owner walks through a door.

Fix: an allowlist that keeps hanging entities, display entities, leash knots
and tamed animals owned by the room owner; capture with entities included.

### B7. Deep Diver bounty is unreachable in the normal loop *(Low, Confirmed)*

It requires `record.floorIndex >= floorsPerSafeVisit * 2` at settlement
(`RunLifecycle.java:1330`), but a safe staging room is forced whenever
`floorIndex % floorsPerSafeVisit == 0` (`EndlessMineRules.java:76-82`), so
settlement always happens at exactly 3. Only an Endless Mine run, which needs
an undisclosed Cube recipe, can progress a weekly bounty that every player is
shown.

Related: `CLEAR_HALLS` and `TIDY` count only the last floor's spawners
(`RunLifecycle.java:1304-1311`, `1326`), not the interval's.

### B8. Stale and misleading copy *(Low, Confirmed)*

- `DialogScreens.java:242`: "Beat the clock to trade up." The clock no longer
  trades anything.
- `DungeonScreen.java:224-226`: the door screen prints `Time: m:ss` once, at
  commit, and never repaints it. A frozen clock with no consequence.
- `RunLifecycle.java:497-502`: "beat the clock for the best reward room" (the
  admin untimed path, but operators read it too).
- `TaskTracker`: "Spend Emeralds at Kadala" names a station nobody in game
  calls Kadala.
- The Start Dungeon tooltip says "Requires a keystone in your main hand";
  `Keystone.findHeld` accepts one anywhere in the inventory.

## 3. UI

The mod's physical UI (selector doors, copper bulbs, lever, text displays) is
its best idea: it is diegetic, it works on vanilla clients, and it makes the
staging room feel like a place. The problems are all about *what* it shows.

### U1. Show omen. It is the game now.

Omen replaced the clock as the loop's pressure (M48, M65), but it has no
display surface at all: no references in `DungeonScreen` or `DialogScreens`,
and no chat line when it rises. Players cannot learn a system they cannot see.

Proposal, all vanilla and server-side:

- A **boss bar** per instance (vanilla `ServerBossEvent`, the hook `RunTimer`
  used to own): title "Omen 2/4 | Interval: 5 | 2 chests, +1 level", colour
  green, yellow, red by band, progress = interval sum against the next band
  threshold.
- A **one-line, in-voice cue** when a source adds omen ("The walls notice you
  lingering.", "Something below heard that."), plus a low `Chime`. This is
  also the only way players will discover dwell omen.
- The **completion message** names the band it landed in and why.

### U2. Replace the clock surfaces

Delete the door screen's `Time:` line and the stale copy in B8. If a time
readout is wanted for the Speedrunner bounty, show elapsed time, not a
countdown that implies a deadline.

### U3. Move progress out of chat

"Trial spawner cleared: 3/8" posts to chat once per spawner, and the pad
refusal is another chat line. Both belong on the boss bar or the action bar
("Spawners 5/8, need 6"). Chat should carry events a player might want to
scroll back to, not a running counter.

### U4. Make the door screen explain the choice

The preview shows level, theme and affixes. It does not show what matters for
the decision: which floor of the interval this is ("Floor 2 of 3"), what the
door banks at the next safe visit, and the fuel cost against the balance. The
engine screen already holds the balance; fold it into the door preview so the
decision is on one surface.

### U5. Group the lodestone menu

Seven flat buttons in each context (Leave, Quit Door, Manage Room, Change
Shell, Stations, Inspect Keystone, Diaries). Vanilla dialogs support nested
screens and the router already handles Back. Three top level entries (Play,
Room, Journal) would halve the first screen.

### U6. Rejoin puts you back

See B3. The single most frustrating moment in the current flow is logging back
in to be told your dungeon closed, when it did not.

## 4. Game loop

The loop as built: home, staging room, pick one of three doors (preview, then
lever), clear 75% of spawners, touch the pad, repeat for three floors, forced
safe door, settle keystone and chests by omen band. The Endless Mine is the
same loop with no forced safe door and a `/dungeon cashout` command.

What works: the preview window is a strong decision moment; the silent room
relocation is a genuine "wait, what?" hook; the omen band is a better stake
than a clock.

What undermines it:

1. **The stakes are hidden** (U1) and **partly broken** (B1, B4). The player
   cannot see omen, the sum carries over by accident, and only the last door
   counts.
2. **The exit rules are three different systems.** A forced safe door every
   three floors, `/dungeon cashout` for the Mine only, and `/dungeon quit`
   meaning "penalty and reset" mid-floor but "plain exit" between floors (and
   between floors, plain exit forfeits the interval, B3). Players will learn
   one rule; there are three.
3. **Parties are fragile.** The owner's connection is a single point of
   failure (R6), and non-owners get no credit (B2) and no guided progress
   (door selection and commit progress only the owner's tasks).
4. **Time does nothing.** A floor can be walked at any pace except in cells
   with an unsolved spawner or lock, where dwell omen accrues invisibly.

Recommended shape, which section 5.2 turns into a concrete replacement:

- **Every staging room offers "go home and bank".** Omen accumulates across
  the floors of an interval; each extra floor adds a depth bonus to the
  payout. Going home settles and resets. The forced safe door becomes a
  default (say, the lever glows after floor 3), not a wall.
- **The Endless Mine becomes a theme and a recipe**, not a second rule set:
  it is just the ordinary loop with a steeper depth bonus and a harsher omen
  curve.
- **Leaving, disconnecting or dying at a checkpoint banks the interval at one
  band worse** instead of forfeiting it. The cost is real, the loss is not
  total, and B3 disappears.
- **Bank per floor cleared**, so each door choice matters on its own floor.

## 6. Mixins, now that the budget is relaxed

`CONVENTIONS.md` still says "exactly one mixin class"; the tree has four
(`CustomClickMixin`, `BlockItemPlaceMixin`, `ResultSlotMixin`,
`ServerExplosionMixin`). Update the rule to what you now want. Cheap, well
targeted wins it would unlock:

- **Tame hook** (`TamableAnimal.tame`): replaces the per-member entity scan
  every watch interval for `TAME_WOLF` (`Instances.java:2130-2137`).
- **Item pickup / container move hook**: lets keystone reconcile run on the
  event instead of a scan over every player's inventory and ender chest every
  second, and would let 5.6 drop the item scan entirely.
- **A room-scoped death hook at the right priority**: today `ALLOW_DEATH`
  cancels death and then hand-resets health, effects and fire. A targeted
  inject into the death path would let totems and other mods' revive effects
  behave normally inside a dungeon.

Keep each one a named file with its target, injection point and the event
route it replaced, as the roadmap suggested; the discipline was good even if
the number was arbitrary.

## 7. Tests

The suite is large and mostly pure-function tests, which is why the state
bugs above got through. `FloorLoopGameTest` checks the transition table and
omen band arithmetic; it never runs a loop. Add three scenario gametests
before any refactor, since they pin the behaviour the refactor must keep:

1. Two full safe visits back to back with a fixed non-zero omen per floor:
   assert the band on visit 2 equals visit 1 (catches B1).
2. Two players touching the pad: assert both have a completion and a run
   record (catches B2).
3. Owner disconnects in `FLOOR_CLEARED` and reconnects: assert they are back in
   the staging room with the interval intact (catches B3).

## 10. Rebuilding from scratch: an estimate

Asked as: how quickly could a fresh version be built, given the goals and the
current code base to read from.

**What carries over untouched:** the data (61 rooms, 58 structure templates,
53 chest loot tables, themes, adventures, affixes, bags, recipes, diaries), the
JSON schemas, `DISCOVERIES.md`'s verified 26.2 API findings, and the generator
(`LayoutGraphGenerator`, `RoomSelector`, the stampers, `BedrockEnvelope`),
which is the strongest part of the code. These are most of the hard-won
knowledge in the project.

**What would be rewritten:** the lifecycle core (`Instances`, `RunLifecycle`,
`InstanceRecord`, `RunSession`, `InstanceTeardown`, about 6,500 lines), custody
(`InventorySwap` and its sidecars), the UI layer, and the economy, all to the
simplified shape in section 5.

| Phase | Scope | Rough size | My working sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Core: slot grid, `RunState` loop, home/staging/floor, settlement, custody with a persistent dungeon inventory, persistence, rejoin | 4,000 to 6,000 lines | 4 to 6 |
| 2 | Port and clean the generator and manifests, content reload | mostly moved, not written | 2 to 3 |
| 3 | Content systems: affixes, omen and its sources, locks and situations, trial content | 5,000 to 7,000 lines | 4 to 6 |
| 4 | UI (screens, omen bar, dialogs), visits, the consolidated economy, Cube recipes | 3,000 to 5,000 lines | 3 to 5 |
| 5 | Scenario gametests, then live verification with you on a client | | open ended |

That is roughly **15 to 20 sessions of my work for about 15,000 to 20,000
lines**, against 43,000 today. In calendar terms, **two to four weeks** if you
playtest between sessions. The code is the fast part; live verification is the
bottleneck, because I can run server gametests but not a client, and every
earlier milestone found its real bugs in your live passes.

**My recommendation is not to start from scratch.** A full rewrite spends half
its time re-deriving what already works (the generator, the content, the 26.2
traps) and carries a real risk of reintroducing the 65 bugs `BUGS.md` already
closed. The rot is concentrated in the lifecycle half. A **targeted rebuild of
the lifecycle core** behind the three scenario tests (order steps 1, 4 and 5
above) gets most of the simplification for about a third of the effort:
roughly 5 to 8 sessions, with the generator, content and data left exactly as
they are.
