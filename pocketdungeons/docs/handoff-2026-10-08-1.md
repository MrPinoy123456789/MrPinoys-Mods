## Fixes, in priority order

1. PD-181 (HIGH, design first): the three-random-door front offer is
   rejected. Owner wants to choose the act and dungeon directly ("players
   should be able to choose the act and dungeon"; his top pick for "first
   thing you'd change"). Propose a design before coding: a picker over
   unlocked acts and dungeons with per-dungeon status (uncleared, cleared,
   capstone), keeping the per-floor door deals inside the trip. Decide what
   happens to echo-shard/life costs and the affix system. Wait for owner
   sign-off.
2. PD-179 (MEDIUM): after `dungeon_finished` auto-banks the haul, the GO
   HOME board reads "Haul 0 scrap" and players think it is a bug. Show the
   scrap just earned ("Banked N scrap") instead of a zeroed haul. Related
   open question from the owner: should the finish auto-bank at all, or
   should the lever always be the payout ritual? Check with the owner.
3. PD-178 (MEDIUM): witch poison still lasts ~30 s; owner wants a 10 s cap.
   Find where poison is applied to dungeon players and clamp the duration.
4. PD-180 (LOW): the dungeon-finished screen's "Home 4 chests" line is
   unreadable. Reword per the board wording rules.
5. PD-190 (MEDIUM, design): the player never reads run chat live; it is a
   log. Audit which critical state is chat-only (floor pay, key redemption,
   omen, milestone fanfare) and move it to titles, the omen bar, the action
   bar or boards. Ties to PD-187.
6. PD-187 (MEDIUM, design): build the persistent display. Owner accepted
   "persistent text should be more than enough": floor info and
   chart/scrap info on a persistent surface (scoreboard sidebar or text
   display entity at the Doors). Propose the mechanism first.
7. PD-184 (MEDIUM, design): capstone floors end flat; "There should be
   some extra challenge in the last floor." Propose options (per-dungeon
   capstone ordeal, themed miniboss, horde wave) and wait for sign-off.
8. PD-183 (MEDIUM, design): unify sculk into one stealth language
   (sensors and shriekers both mean "be quiet or it gets worse") and
   replace `sensor_gallery` with a room built on that rule.
9. PD-182 (MEDIUM, design): scrap curve. Owner proposal: each level
   requires more scrap than the previous; higher floors pay more. Tune
   constants; keep haul mechanics unchanged. Coordinate with PD-181's
   economy decisions if it lands first.
10. PD-186 (LOW, design): the hopper-key toll room was named worst room of
    the session. Identify whether the complaint is the barred_vault/
    ominous_bargain SpurToll rooms or Frame Lock, and whether the problem
    is readability, fiction or the room itself.
11. PD-185 (LOW, content): Copper Works mobs should wear two copper-themed
    equipment pieces including weapons, scoped to that dungeon.
12. PD-188 (LOW, design): Feral affix feels out of place. Owner wants a
    wolf/pet themed dungeon or floor, a rare generic wolf room, and a new
    affix in Feral's slot.
13. PD-189 (LOW, design): mine-flavoured floors set an ore expectation the
    content does not meet. Extend `hiddenOre` to Endless Mine (and any
    other mine-themed dungeon) or tune room ore density
    (deep_shaft_landing was called out as thin).
14. PD-176 follow-up (harness): the watcher auto-thinks on every ask now,
    but replies that arrive after the 45 s fallback still journal
    `llm_late` (five this session) and one ask went fully unanswered when
    a reply attached to a sibling ask only. Harness fix, not mod: make
    `lemonwatch.mjs` send a canned hold reply itself on each ask, and check
    that a reply does not drop other pending asks.
