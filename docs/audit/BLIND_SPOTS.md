# Blind spots: what the owner did not ask about

Ranked by how much the owner would regret missing each one. Evidence and sizes are in `FINDINGS.md` under the ids given.

1. **Minors, logs and privacy (OP-15, LM-1).** The Discord study says minors play and the server's own rules say so. Ledger keeps every block action for 90 days, `log-ips=true` logs real addresses (Bedrock households share one), Charter Currency analytics and Charter history keep player UUIDs and names indefinitely with no retention note, Lemon journals what players type per account forever, and a Lord with a one hour Ledger grant reads the whole server's history. There is no privacy statement anywhere a player reads and no way to honour a deletion request. Regret: a parent asks what you keep about their child and the honest answer is "everything, forever". Fix: a short privacy note in the Server Manual, a purge command for analytics, a 7 day Lemon retention, Ledger grants logged with reason. Two hours plus a decision on retention.

2. **One person deep (OP-1, OP-2, OP-4, OP-16).** The whole server, its deploys, its backups, its unpushed source and every approval run through the owner's PC and the owner's chat. No co-owner holds panel access; no restore has ever been done; nothing alerts anyone. Regret: a week of holiday during which the server crashes on day two. Fix: push master, a Pterodactyl sub-user for a co-owner, a restore drill, a scheduled status post to Discord. A day.

3. **Entity griefing with no attribution (CF-1, FO-4, CF-10).** Blocks in kingdom land are protected; horses, villagers, golems, pets, frames and armour stands are not, against arrows, tridents, potions, dispensers, wind charges or a Dwarf's Ground Slam in the kingdom's own keep. Ledger logs block changes, not arrows. Regret: the first grief ticket is "someone shot all our villagers" and nobody can say who. Fix in `DO_NOW.md` tasks 5 and 12.

4. **The tests pass and prove the wrong things (CC-10, EC-7, CF-16).** Every suite is green. INVARIANTS.md claims fuzz coverage of the war book and the siege clock that does not exist; Charter Currency, the mod that holds the money, has no gametest; the README's load test does not exist; the Fortune gametests use a fake bank. Green builds give confidence the code has not earned in exactly the places players will push. Fix: the gametest module and the fuzz generator additions in `DO_NOW.md` tasks 6, 7, 9.

5. **A config file is a loaded gun (OP-10, CF-3, OP-12).** One bad value in `charter.json` silently runs every knob on defaults, which unlocks governance; a lore line in the wrong shape in `config/foundry/content` stops the server from booting (FO-3); eleven config folders have three different missing-key policies; player balances live in the config folder where a configs-only restore overwrites them (OP-13). Regret: a late-night edit before bed and a server that boots wrong, or not at all, while the owner sleeps. Fix in tasks 3, 12, 17.

6. **The next Minecraft version is a six-mod cliff (XM-9, CF-9, FO-7, AU-1).** 59 required mixins across six mods, all `defaultRequire 1`: one renamed target and the server does not start. Two docs say Foundry has no mixins; it has five. Builds depend on a Floodgate SNAPSHOT. There is no playbook. Geyser and Floodgate lag each release, and half the players are Bedrock. Regret: Mojang ships, players update, the server cannot follow for weeks. Fix: `UPGRADE_PLAYBOOK.md`, remove Trinkets and GoldGate first, pin a released Floodgate API, one version file at the root.

7. **Performance has never been measured (XM-10, FO-8, PD-W1, PD-W3).** About fifteen handlers touch every player at least once a second, three walk every inventory every five seconds, one walks every entity in the world every five seconds, senses read up to 7,200 blocks per Fae every two seconds, race looks send about 27,000 entity packets a second in a 30 player hub, and Pocket Dungeons generates overworld chunks synchronously and stamps whole dungeons in one tick. Ten GB and six cores is fine for vanilla at 85 players; nobody knows what this stack costs. Regret: "the server lags" with no idea which of thirty handlers it is. Fix: one spark profile at an evening peak before changing anything; then the PD fixes in task 13.

8. **ViaVersion and older clients (XM-6).** Live since 2026-11-04, in no document. Charter's menus are 26.x dialogs; a 1.21.x client may get no menu at all, which makes `requireAffiliation` a locked door for them. Regret: a new player on an older client stands in spawn forever and nobody knows why. Fix: a minimum client version in the manual and MOTD; one test with an older client.

9. **Trinkets, GoldGate and Lemon are dead weight that can still hurt (XM-3, XM-5, LM-3, LM-4).** Trinkets and its Polymer patch are installed while Foundry empties every trinket slot every ten ticks; GoldGate overrides a vanilla loot table that can never fire with the Nether off; Lemon answers every question with "I do not know that one yet." and, if `chatTalk` is ever turned on, swallows the chat of any player standing alone. Regret: a port that fights three jars for nothing, and a baffled player base. Fix: remove two jars at a restart; hide Lemon behind a knob.

10. **Moderation has no appeal and no cross-tool view (OP-17, OP-18).** Lords are the only moderators and they can mute, bar and kick their citizens with the reason journaled in Charter; BanHammer, LuckPerms and Ledger keep their own logs; the LuckPerms groups built for staff have no members; a muted citizen's only path is Discord. The Discord study already shows two public warnings and a ban request in week one. Regret: a Lord abusing the power is found weeks later by reading a log. Fix: an appeal line in the mute message, `/charter admin moderation` filtered by Lord, a weekly digest to the staff channel, the two admins put in LuckPerms.

11. **The economy has no model of alts (EC-10, EC-5).** Xbox accounts are free; Floodgate UUIDs are stable per account; five minutes of playtime is the only gate. Each alt earns Renown daily through Fortune; a Java plus Bedrock pair of one person gets two spins. Regret: a kingdom's Renown ladder won by whoever owns the most Xbox accounts. Fix: `renownMultiplier 0.5` on the `fortune` source, or a per kingdom per source daily cap.

12. **Documentation that disagrees with itself (OP-19, OP-6).** Axiom is enabled in two guides and disabled in the newest handoff; operators are seven in one file and two in another; the deploy quirks described no longer exist; three 2026-08 documents about a different server sit in `docs/` unmarked. Regret: the Devin or agent the owner hands this to acts on the wrong facts. Fix: `docs/README.md` as the one entry point, two corrected lines, three files archived.

13. **Accessibility beyond Bedrock.** Colour is the only cue in the kingdom tag colours, the keep border glow and the action bar states; the action bar is shared by six systems and overwrites itself within two seconds (UX-12); 24 character labels on a TV are small; nothing is read aloud. Nobody asked; nobody checked. Regret: small, until a player says they cannot tell kingdoms apart. Fix: a letter or symbol next to each colour in tags, the action bar yield rule.

14. **Secrets at rest (OP-14).** Git and logs are clean. The Discord bot token sits in plain text in `config/charter/discord.json` inside every panel backup and every off-site tarball; the panel key on the owner's PC has full power over the server; Xbox accounts have held operator level 4. Regret: a shared backup leaks a bot that can post as the server. Fix: rotate after any backup leaves the owner's hands; IP-restrict the key.

15. **Legal and platform.** Nothing here is a problem today, but nobody has written down: the Minecraft EULA position on anything Fortune sells (the stake is coin for Valor and items, no real money, which is fine); the Discord bot's data handling; that the server has no age statement while hosting minors and swearing staff (the Discord study's section 11). Regret: low probability, high embarrassment. Fix: one paragraph in the Server Manual.
