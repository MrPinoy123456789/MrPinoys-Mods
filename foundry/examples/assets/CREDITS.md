## Everything else

The other models and textures are generated by `foundry/tools/gen_trinket_models.py`: the cuboid shapes are our own, and
the textures are crops of, and swatches taken from, the project's own flat item sprites in `kingdoms/textures/item/`.
They carry no outside licence. When an outside asset is added, record here: file name, source URL, author, licence,
what was changed.
