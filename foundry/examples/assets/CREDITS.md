# Asset credits

No third party assets are used in the Kingdoms worn models.

- Models `kingdoms/models/item/worn/*.json` and textures `kingdoms/textures/item/worn/*.png` are generated by
  `foundry/tools/gen_trinket_models.py` from numbers in that script. The cape, pauldron, circlet and gills textures are
  crops of, and colour swatches from, the project's own flat sprites in `kingdoms/textures/item/`. The fairy wings
  texture is painted by the script (`paint_wings`). They carry no outside licence.
- Checked but not used: "Fairy Wings Elytra" by Kchem and Garden Gals (Modrinth, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Its wing texture is
  made to wrap an elytra cuboid, so on a flat wing plate it showed as a speckled rectangle; it was dropped, and no file
  from it is in the repository.

Code ideas followed, not copied: the way a display follows the player (a transformation matrix from the body yaw and
pose each tick, with interpolation and teleport duration) follows Patbox's Polymer entity model handlers, for example
`SnailEntityModelHandler` in https://github.com/PolymerPorts/lovely-snails-patch (LGPL-3.0).

When an outside asset is added, record here: file name, source URL, author, licence, and what was changed.
