# Asset credits

No third party assets are used in the Kingdoms worn models. The models in `kingdoms/models/item/worn/` and the
textures in `kingdoms/textures/worn/` are generated by `foundry/tools/gen_trinket_models.py` from numbers in that
script, so they carry no outside licence. The flat item sprites in `kingdoms/textures/item/` are the project's own
placeholder art. If an outside asset is added later, record here: file name, source URL, author, licence, and what was
changed.
